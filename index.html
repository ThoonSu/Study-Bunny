Pomodoro Technique

The Pomodoro technique helps you study better by breaking your work into 25-minute "sprints" followed by short breaks. This method stops you from feeling overwhelmed because it’s much easier to focus for a short time than for several hours. The breaks act like a quick recharge for your brain, keeping you energized and preventing burnout. By using a timer, you stay away from distractions and turn your study time into small, winning moments.
