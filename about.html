Behind the Burrow

We believe that learning shouldn't feel like a chore. Study Bunny was created to transform the stress of studying into a cozy, rewarding experience.

Our Mission

Traditional productivity tools are often cold and clinical. At Study Bunny, we use gamified rewards and pastel aesthetics to lower study anxiety.

By giving you a virtual companion to care for, your progress becomes visible. Every flashcard mastered isn't just a fact learned—it's a step toward a happier, more stylish bunny.

Who belongs in the Burrow?

Students

From middle school to PhD candidates, we help you crush your exams without the usual burnout.

Lifelong Learners

Mastering a new language or hobby? Keep your knowledge organized in a space that feels like home.

Cozy Seekers

For those who value aesthetics as much as action. We make productivity look and feel "hoppy."
